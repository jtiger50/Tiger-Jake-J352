As economic concerns grow, investors turn to gold

Gold futures reached an all-time high in September with unemployment climbing


By Jake Tiger

November 24, 2025


Gold, at nearly $3,500 an ounce in September, is trading at its highest price on record as investors sense a possible recession with the unemployment rate also on the rise.

The price of gold rises when investors begin preparing for a recession and would rather put their money in something safer. As a result, gold has a positive correlation with recession indicators like the unemployment rate.
